Benchmark results

Social network — Stanford SNAP CollegeMsg

1,899 users, 59,835 temporal interactions; 40 common active users selected for controlled-perturbation matching (L = 30).

50–100%Proposed accuracy across 0–30% perturbation
56–58%Fewer optimization iterations vs. baseline

Drosophila brain — Hemibrain vs. FlyWire/FAFB

Mushroom body ROI compared across two independent flies (~4.4K vs. ~9.9K neurons); tested on statistically matched ~300-neuron subgraphs due to full-graph scale.

6.1×Faster (83s → 14s), identical match quality

MOF crystal screening — QMOF database

20,372 DFT-relaxed structures screened for the Zr₆ bridging-carboxylate motif; Stage 1 filters chemistry, Stage 2 verifies geometry (RMSD ≤ 2.0 Å).

42×Rank-1 factorization speedup at N=500
1.000 AUCStage 1 chemistry separation
7.3%Pass Stage 2 geometric verification
~53 minFull 20,372-structure sweep, standard laptop
