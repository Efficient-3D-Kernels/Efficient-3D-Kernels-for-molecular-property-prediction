Theoretical foundations — summary

The team's internal paper establishes the following results underlying the implementation:

  1. Lemma 1 (Rank-One Factorization): the co-matching indicator U = X·1·Xᵀ simplifies to a rank-one outer product r·rᵀ, cutting its computation from O(MN² + M²N) to O(MN + M²).
  2. Theorem 1 (Structural Gradient): a closed-form gradient for the Hadamard-product structural mismatch term, avoiding the need for numerical differentiation.
  3. Theorem 2 (Frank-Wolfe Duality Gap): a certified suboptimality bound used for early termination during optimization.
  4. Proposition 4 (Frobenius Normalization): scale-invariant adjacency normalization so structural cost stays comparable across graphs of very different size and edge weight, from 40-node social graphs to 500-atom crystal structures.
  5. Theorem 4 (Kabsch Alignment): the closed-form O(n) 3D rigid alignment used in Stage 2, reusing the Stage 1 correspondence directly.
  6. Theorem 3 (Per-Iteration Complexity): an operation-level complexity analysis showing the pipeline's cost is dominated by the bilinear term and the Hungarian assignment step, Θ(n³) for M ≈ N.
